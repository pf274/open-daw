Alignment / recording

Re-align replaces notes inside the take's time range. Undo restores them.

How to use

1. Click 🎤 Mic and whistle; the readout shows the detected note.
2. Select a layer, put the playhead where you want to start, press ● Rec (or R).
3. After the count-in, whistle the melody and tap any key on each note. Esc stops.
4. Fix notes in the roll, add layers, export MIDI.

Tap-rhythm mode (no mic): press 🥁 Tap rhythm (or T), tap the beat, Esc. Then the keyboard becomes a piano: press keys to audition, hold one for 0.75 s to lock it in and move to the next note.

Roll: double-click adds a note · drag moves · drag right edge resizes · drag on empty space box-selects · Shift-click adds to the selection · Alt-drag duplicates.
Ruler: click sets the playhead · Shift-drag sets the loop.
Scroll: wheel = pitch, Shift+wheel = time, Ctrl+wheel = zoom time, Ctrl+Shift+wheel = zoom pitch.
Use headphones when recording over other layers.
